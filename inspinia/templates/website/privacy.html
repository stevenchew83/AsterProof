Privacy Policy.

How AsterProof handles the information you share.

Updated 30 October 2026

Who operates AsterProof

AsterProof is operated by {{ public_operator_name }}. For questions about your information or to make a privacy request, email {{ public_contact_email }}.

Information we use

When you create an account, we process your email address, authentication information, and profile details you choose to provide. Using the workspace can create records of your learning activity, problem completions, solutions, submissions, and shared lists.

When you request newsletter updates, we store your email address, subscription status, and timestamps recording your request, confirmation, and any unsubscribe action. Subscription is optional and separate from your account.

The service also uses session and security records and technical request information, such as network addresses, browser information, and page activity, to operate the website, investigate problems, and protect against abuse.

Why we use it

We use this information to provide accounts and learning tools, manage account access, deliver requested account emails, respond to support requests, and maintain the service. We send newsletter updates only after you request them and confirm your email.

Newsletter choice

Entering an email address does not subscribe it immediately. Confirmation links expire after 48 hours, and confirming requires an action on the website. You can unsubscribe using the preference link in our newsletter emails, or contact {{ public_operator_name }}. Unsubscribing from updates does not stop necessary account or security emails.

Public content and shared lists

Library pages are public when deliberately published. Problem lists marked public can be viewed by anyone with their share link, including their displayed author credit and list content. Consider what you include before sharing a list.

Shared solutions

Creating a public solution link shares a copy of your saved solution, its problem statement and topics, and any author name or date enabled in your saved display options. Anyone with the link can read it and forward it. Your account email and personal progress are not included. Later editor changes stay separate until you update the shared version. You can stop sharing while retaining your saved solution; sharing again creates a new link.

Shared solution pages are marked not to be indexed and are not listed in AsterProof’s public catalogue. Link-preview services may retrieve the page title, description, and image when someone shares the link. Copies, screenshots, and previews already held by recipients or other services cannot be recalled by stopping sharing.

Service providers and transfers

We use hosting and email providers to operate AsterProof, including AWS hosting and Google/Gmail for email. These providers may process information outside your country. Mathematical content may load MathJax from the jsDelivr content-delivery network to display formulas. Such requests disclose ordinary connection information to the provider.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to address misuse, protect the service, or respond to a legitimate legal request.

Cookies and browser storage

Essential cookies support sign-in, sessions, language choices, and form protection. Existing workspace settings may also use browser storage to remember appearance preferences. Solution Writer offers optional recovery copies on your device, available for seven days after the last edit. You can restore or clear them in the editor. Choosing to continue in your workspace temporarily carries your writing in your sign-in session, available for up to seven days; saving the private draft or logging out clears that handoff. The public website does not add advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

Retention and security

We retain information for the operation of the service, account administration, consent records, security, and applicable obligations. A minimal subscription record may be retained after unsubscribing to record your preference. Contact us to request deletion of information that is no longer needed.

We use access controls and encrypted website connections to protect information. Please keep your password and sign-in codes private. No online service can promise complete security.

Your choices and requests

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your information, withdraw newsletter consent, or ask how your information is used by emailing the contact above. We may need to verify that the request is yours before acting. Some information may need to be retained for security, legal requirements, or the rights of others.

Providing information for a newsletter is voluntary. Information needed to create and protect an account is required to use the account features. You can browse the public library without creating an account.

Young learners

If you are a young learner, involve a parent or guardian before creating an account or sharing personal information. Parents and guardians can contact {{ public_operator_name }} with questions or requests about a learner’s information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The update date above identifies the current version. Please contact us if anything needs clarification.
