03 / Every attempt has something to teach

Keep the proof.
Remember the practice.

Save your proof as a draft, then mark the problem solved when you are ready. Your personal progress brings your activity, topics, and difficulty together.

  • Keep your work. Return to drafts, publish a solution when ready, and find your writing in My solutions.
  • Reflect on the attempt. Record time spent, obstacles, confidence, and a date to try again.
  • Choose your next challenge. Explore completed and remaining problems by area and concept.

Progress reflects your recorded practice. Normal draft saves leave progress unchanged. Use Save changes to keep your proof, then separately choose Mark problem solved to update your progress.
