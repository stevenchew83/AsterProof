About AsterProof

A place to think.
A little more deeply.

AsterProof brings a mathematics library and a problem-solving workspace together, with understanding at the centre.

The idea

Mathematics is a collection of connections.

A technique becomes useful when you see what it is doing. A proof becomes memorable when you understand the idea holding it together.

AsterProof is being built around those moments: a well-chosen explanation, a surprising relationship, and the satisfaction of finding your own way through a problem.

A library with a learning path.

The library is organised into Algebra, Geometry, Number theory, and Combinatorics. Each subject opens into areas, concepts, and lectures. The structure is ready; materials will appear as they are prepared and published.

A workspace for deliberate practice.

Your account gives you a place to work with olympiad problems, write solutions, organise problem lists, and reflect on your progress. Public shared lists keep useful collections within reach of other learners.
