How coverage is counted

Active problem statements take priority. Active archive problems without a statement are included. Linked entries share one problem identity and count once. Statement tags take priority over linked archive tags.

Concepts use the shared mathematical catalogue. Aliases and different roles for the same concept count once per problem. Concepts with no matching problems remain visible as coverage gaps. Median MOHS uses only problems with a known difficulty.

Subject, area, contest, and year define this view. Search, coverage tabs, and sorting refine the concept explorer. Library coverage does not measure a learner’s mastery.
