How progress is counted

Each problem counts once in an area or concept, even when it has several aliases or roles. Subject groups follow the mathematical concept catalogue. A problem can contribute to more than one area or subject, so their counts can overlap.

Solved, checked, written, and published records count as completed. Attempted and unattempted records remain open. A statement completion takes priority over a linked archive completion.

Concepts practiced have at least one completed problem. This measures recorded practice and does not establish mastery. Areas or concepts without problems have no completion percentage.

Progress reflects current library mappings and recorded completions.{% if unmapped_total %} {{ unmapped_total }} active problems in this subject scope have no mapped concepts and are excluded from these progress counts.{% endif %} Search and status tabs refine the explorer; summary figures follow the selected subject and area.
